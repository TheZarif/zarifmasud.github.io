title: "Sinophobia on Twitter: Integrating Theory, Humans, and Machines"
authors: "Ahana Biswas, Zarif Masud, Shebuti Rayana, Syed Ishtiaque Ahmed"
category: manuscripts
venue: "ACM CSCW Workshop on Addressing Challenges and Opportunities in Online Extremism Research"
year: 2021
---
This workshop paper explores Sinophobic discourse on Twitter by integrating social theory, human annotation, and machine learning. It highlights the value of interdisciplinary approaches for studying online extremism and racialized hate.
